pockets of greatness

chunk · B2 · professional · acknowledging · framing

Original context

Steve Jobs - Get Much Simpler, Be Really Clear - Sept. 23, 1997

We’re trying to get back to the basics of great products, great marketing, and great distribution.

And I think that that Apple has has pockets of greatness, but in some ways has drifted away from from doing the basics really well.

So, we started with the product line.

Jobs 先肯定苹果仍有零散的强项，再用 but 转到整体偏离基础的问题，这是商业场合里“先让步、再指出问题”的典型开场。记住它的搭配：have pockets of greatness (in …)，后面通常紧跟一个转折。

Meaning

名词性短语，指整体平庸之中零散出现的优秀之处。

字面义是“greatness 的口袋”。在这里，说话人承认苹果仍有局部的强项，但整体已经偏离了做好基础业务的核心。语气是委婉让步，既肯定了优点，也为后面的批评做铺垫。

Use it

[team / company] has pockets of greatness in [area], but [problem].

team / company被评价的对象，如 the team、our product
area有强项的领域，如 design、delivery
problem转折后指出的整体问题

常见搭配 · have / find pockets of greatness · pockets of greatness in [area]

Pronunciation

/ˈpɒkɪts əv ˈɡreɪtnəs/POCK-ets of GREAT-ness

口语里 of 弱读为 /əv/，和前后的 pockets、greatness 自然连读；重音落在 pockets 和 greatness 的第一个音节。

Similar expressions

islands of excellence语义几乎完全对等，更常见于书面报告
scattered bright spots更口语化，指零散的亮点
isolated areas of strength更正式，强调孤立存在的优势领域

Examples

The old system has pockets of greatness, but it is too slow for modern needs. typical

这套旧系统仍有一些零散的优秀之处，但对现代需求来说太慢了。

The team has pockets of greatness in design, but struggles with consistent delivery.

这个团队在设计领域有局部突出的优势，但在稳定交付方面存在困难。

Despite the company’s recent struggles, there are pockets of greatness worth preserving.

尽管这家公司近期陷入困境，仍有一些值得保留的局部优势。

Role in the sentence

宾语从句里第一个并列谓语 has 的宾语。

所在句主干是 I think [that Apple has pockets of greatness, but in some ways has drifted away …]。从句内部由 but 连接两个并列谓语，两个谓语的主语都是 Apple。
